## 1. Release 1
Foundation release: authentication, workspace concepts, source-of-truth records, dashboards, and operational audit.

Planned sprints:
- Sprint 1: Platform foundations
- Sprint 2: Workflow engine hardening
- Sprint 3: Dashboard and notifications

## 2. Release 2
Operational release: task engine, assignment engine, search, and richer workspace views.

Planned sprints:
- Sprint 1: Task lifecycle
- Sprint 2: Assignment and workload management
- Sprint 3: Search and filters

## 3. Release 3
Governance release: reporting, compliance views, stronger policy enforcement, and richer auditability.

Planned sprints:
- Sprint 1: Compliance registers
- Sprint 2: Reporting and review
- Sprint 3: Audit and controls

## 4. Release 4
Scale release: multi-workspace hardening, shared platform services, onboarding patterns, and improved administrative controls.

Planned sprints:
- Sprint 1: Multi-workspace policies
- Sprint 2: Admin tooling
- Sprint 3: Reusable platform modules

## 5. Release 5
Intelligence release: AI-assisted triage, document intelligence, recommendation surfaces, and future channel integrations.

Planned sprints:
- Sprint 1: AI readiness and advisories
- Sprint 2: Document intelligence
- Sprint 3: Future messaging integrations

## 6. Future Vision
TEOS becomes a reusable operating platform for Torque Empire and related entities, capable of supporting multiple business domains without redesigning the base architecture.
