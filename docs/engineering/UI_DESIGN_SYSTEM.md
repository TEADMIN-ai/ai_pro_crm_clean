## 1. Design Intent
TEOS interfaces must feel like enterprise operational software: clear, dense, usable, and calm. Visual treatment should support repeated daily work rather than marketing presentation.

## 2. Colour System
- Corporate blue for primary actions and active states.
- White and grey for surfaces and spacing.
- Dark text for readability.
- Red reserved for critical errors and destructive risk.

## 3. Typography
- Use a clean, highly legible sans-serif system.
- Prioritize readable contrast and stable sizing.
- Active labels should be bold and unambiguous.

## 4. Spacing and Layout
- Prefer predictable spacing tokens.
- Use stable card and table dimensions.
- Avoid decorative clutter that competes with operational content.

## 5. Status Colours
- Green: Completed
- Blue: In Progress
- Amber: Pending
- Orange: Review Required
- Red: Critical
- Grey: Not Started

## 6. Components
- Buttons: primary, secondary, destructive, disabled.
- Tabs: clearly selected and inactive states.
- Tables: readable headers, row spacing, and scan-friendly hierarchy.
- Forms: visible labels, focus states, and legible inputs.

## 7. Accessibility
- Meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and interaction standards.
- Never rely on colour alone to convey state.
- Ensure keyboard, screen reader, and mobile usability.

## 8. Motion and Feedback
- Motion should reinforce state change, not distract.
- Loading and progress feedback should be purposeful and lightweight.
