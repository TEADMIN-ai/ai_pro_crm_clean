# QS Guide

QS Phase 3 Foundation is complete and feature-frozen for v1.0 RC.

## Supported Workflows

- BOQ upload/review.
- Estimate creation and detail review.
- Supplier intelligence.
- Supplier offers and recommendations.
- Commercial impact scenarios.
- Commercial intelligence summary.
- Learning feedback foundation and price history foundation.

## Release Rules

- Do not add major QS features during RC hardening.
- Use real supplier/material data only.
- Label insufficient data honestly.
- Do not claim ML training unless real training has run.

## QA Checks

- Open QA estimate.
- Confirm recommendations load when offers exist.
- Confirm missing data produces honest empty states.
- Confirm commercial dashboard does not show fake forecasting.
